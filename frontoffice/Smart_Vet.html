FAQ

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign up", choose pet owner or veterinarian and fill in the form.

How do I book a veterinarian?

Open "Find a vet", filter by city and specialty, then book.

How do I add my pet?

Go to "My pets" and click "Add an animal".

How does adoption work?

Open "Adopt", pick an animal and send a request.
